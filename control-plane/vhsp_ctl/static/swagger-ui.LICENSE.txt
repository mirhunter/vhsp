swagger-ui-bundle.js, swagger-ui-standalone-preset.js, and swagger-ui.css
are the pre-built distribution of Swagger UI (swagger-ui-dist on npm,
version 5.32.11 at the time this was vendored -- see
vhsp_ctl/static/README.md for how to update). Vendored, not loaded from
a CDN, matching this project's "no external runtime dependencies"
posture (same pattern as images/tenant-admin/static/'s CodeMirror
bundle).
